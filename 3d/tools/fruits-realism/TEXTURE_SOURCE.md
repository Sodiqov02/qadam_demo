# Fruit skin atlas source

`skin-atlas.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on
2026-09-15 and is used as source art by `build.py`. The generated colors are
embedded into the production GLB; the website has no image-generation runtime
dependency.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single 2048x2048 PBR
> base-color fruit skin texture atlas for a realistic 3D food model. Make
> exactly four equally sized square texture swatches in a precise 2 by 2 grid,
> filling the entire image edge to edge, no gaps, no borders, no text. Every
> quadrant is a perfectly flat orthographic macro photograph of fruit skin
> only, no whole fruit, silhouette, stem, leaves or background. Top-left: ripe
> Gala apple peel with irregular fine crimson streaks over golden yellow-green
> skin and tiny pale lenticels. Top-right: fresh navel orange peel with fine
> uneven pores and subtle tonal mottling. Bottom-left: ripe peach skin with
> dark rose-red blush fading into warm golden cream, fine fuzz and speckles.
> Bottom-right: ripe pomegranate rind with deep garnet, muted crimson, fine
> freckles, pores and mottling. Use even diffuse cross-polarized albedo-style
> lighting: no highlights, cast shadows, directional shading, vignette,
> painted look, artificial noise or geometric pattern.
